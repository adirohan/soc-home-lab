# Reconnaissance

This section documents reconnaissance activities performed against the Windows endpoint in the SOC home lab.

## Objective

The objective of reconnaissance is to identify reachable hosts, exposed services, open ports, and other information that can help understand the attack surface.

## Tools

- Nmap
- Kali Linux

## Target

Windows 11 SOC endpoint

## Reconnaissance Activities

The following activities will be documented:

- Host discovery
- Port scanning
- Service enumeration
- Basic network reconnaissance

## Evidence

Nmap scan results and screenshots will be added here as the reconnaissance exercises are performed.
